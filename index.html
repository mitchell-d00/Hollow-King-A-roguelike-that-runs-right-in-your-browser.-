Hollow King

Twenty floors down, something still wears the crown.

Keys and symbols

Move with the arrow keys or W A S D; Q E Z C move diagonally. Walk into a monster to attack it and into a shopkeeper to trade. Click a monster to target it, or click the map to step that way.

1 to 4 use abilities. F fires a bow, crossbow or wand at your target. X explores automatically. R rests. Period waits. I opens the pack, P the character sheet. Enter or > takes the stairs, or walks to them once you have seen them.
